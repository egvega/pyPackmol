molovol.py in pyPackmol is a Python (NumPy) translation of the molecular
volume algorithm of MoloVol:

  MoloVol - https://github.com/jmaglic/MoloVol
  J. B. Maglic, R. Lavendomme, J. Appl. Crystallogr. 2022, 55, 1033-1044.

Changes made in the translation: the octree was replaced by a dense voxel
grid classified with NumPy array operations; atomic radii are taken from
PyMOL instead of an elements file; the graphical and command-line
interfaces and the surface-area and cavity-analysis functions were not
translated; rotation to the principal axes and skipping of empty grid
blocks were added.
